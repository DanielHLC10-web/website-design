Artistry & Tradition

THE CRAFT OF FIRE & CHILI

At Spice Temple, culinary mastery lies in balancing the intense heat of regional Chinese spice with delicate, fragrant aromatics.

Rooted in ancient techniques from Sichuan, Yunnan, Hunan, and Jiangxi, our kitchen celebrates the legendary Wok Hei—the breath of the wok. Every dish is orchestrated to evoke texture, temperature, and deep complexity.
