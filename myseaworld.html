Dolphin

are smart, playful marine mammals that live in groups called pods. They breathe air through a blowhole on top of their heads and use special clicking sounds, called echolocation, to find food like fish and squid.

Sea Turtle

Sea turtles are ancient, air-breathing reptiles that have swam in the world's oceans for over 100 million years. There are seven distinct species—including the green, leatherback, and hawksbill—most of which inhabit warm tropical and subtropical waters to feed, migrate, and nest on sandy shores

Reef Sharks

Reef sharks are small to medium-sized sharks living around coral reefs. The most common species are the blacktip, whitetip, and grey reef shark. They grow up to 6 feet long, eat fish and crabs, give birth to live pups, and are rarely a threat to people./p> Explore
