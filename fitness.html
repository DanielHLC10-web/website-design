Fitness Routine Cards

Morning stretch

A quick morning stretch wakes up your tight muscles, boosts blood flow, and gets your body ready for the day.

Time: 20mins

Cardio

Cardio exercise raises your heart rate, strengthens your heart, and builds endurance to keep your whole body healthy.

Time: 45 mins

Plyometerics

Plyometrics uses explosive, fast movements—like jump squats or hops—to train your muscles to fire like a spring and build maximum power in a short time.

Time: 30 mins

Weight bearing

Weight-bearing exercise forces your body to work against gravity, helping build strong bones and prevent bone loss.

Time: 1 hour

Recovery

Rest hard, heal strong, and get back to chasing your goals

Time: 45 mins

Rest AND Hydrate

Rest and hydration help your muscles heal and keep your energy high after a hard workout. Drink water and take time to sleep so your body can grow stronger.
